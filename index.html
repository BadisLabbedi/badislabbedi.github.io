About

Talks

2026

Wormholes and The Factorization Puzzle in Quantum Gravity

Blackboard talk for Society of Physics Students

abstract

Recent progress suggested that the ensemble-average-looking  wormhole contributions in the gravitational path integral can by accounted for by averaging the erratic behavior in large N and that this averaging could be a viable solution to the factorization puzzle in Quantum Gravity. In this talk, I will introduce at a very high-level the key concepts of the previous sentence and, time allowing, try to explain recent progress solving this puzzle in a 2d Quantum Gravity.
