Wormholes and Averaging in Quantum Gravity

Blackboard talk outline for an undergraduate audience.

abstract

Gravitational wormholes appear to connect otherwise independent quantum systems. In simple models of two-dimensional gravity, their contributions are naturally described by an ensemble average. This talk introduces the factorization puzzle and the proposal that averaging the erratic behavior of observables as the system size N changes can reproduce gravitational correlations. I will describe the basic matrix-model picture and progress in two-dimensional topological gravity.
